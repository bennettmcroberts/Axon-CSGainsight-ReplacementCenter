How to schedule a session

  1. Confirm the audience and preferred format (onsite vs. virtual) with the customer's training coordinator
  2. Check the course's typical duration above and propose 2–3 candidate dates
  3. Log a CTA (type Onboarding or CS Request) against the account so the session is tracked to completion
  4. After the session, confirm attendance and log it as a communication on the account so future CSMs can see training history at a glance

Requesting a custom session

Large or multi-site agencies sometimes need a tailored session — combining two courses, covering a custom policy workflow, or training a specific unit (e.g. K-9, SWAT) on a specialized use case. Route custom requests through your CSM lead rather than the standard catalog; include the audience size, desired outcomes, and target date so it can be scoped properly.

Why training completion matters for renewal readiness

Agencies with low training completion consistently show lower product adoption and a rockier renewal conversation — undertrained end users are the single most common root cause behind "we're not seeing the value" pushback at renewal time. If an account's renewal readiness checklist shows weak engagement, check training completion first before assuming it's a product or pricing issue.
